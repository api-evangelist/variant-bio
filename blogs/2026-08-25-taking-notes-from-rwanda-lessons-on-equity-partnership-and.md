Photo credit: Sarah LeBaron von Baeyer Ever since Variant Bio first launched its unique benefit-sharing program in 2020 , I have been involved in putting the program into practice, together with researchers around the world, from French Polynesia to Madagascar . In part due to the lack of a clear precedent for how to institutionalize the community-driven distribution of financial benefits in biomedical research , the process is rarely devoid of challenges.
